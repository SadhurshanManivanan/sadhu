What information is in the FASTA header line?
Accession number, organism, sequence type, and description about transcript variation.

What does the accession number format tell you about this record?
NM means RefSeq mRNA, 000546 is the unique identifier, and .6 is the sequence version.

What additional information does the GenBank format provide compared to FASTA?
GenBank provides addiional annotations such as keywords, comments, gene name, and organism.
The gene is TP53, and transcript variant 1 encodes the cellular tumor antigen p53.

How many amino acids does human TP53 have?
Human TP53 has 393 amino acids.

How does the UniProt FASTA header format differ from the NCBI FASTA header?
NCBI FASTA mainly focuses on the accession number and transcript description.
UniProt is more protein-focused and includes structured fields such as GN, PE, OX, and SV.
